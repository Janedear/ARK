Welcome to ARK Crisis Suite

ARK is a fully offline-capable emergency communication and coordination tool. It works without internet, can run from a USB drive, and you can share it instantly with anyone who needs it.

  • Works 100% offline — no servers, no accounts required
  • Send this file via text, email, AirDrop, or USB to your neighbors
  • Coordinate with your neighborhood hub when in range
  • Manage medical triage, resources, and household status
⚡ This app requires no internet. All data is stored only on your device. Nothing is transmitted to any company or server unless you connect to a local hub.

🛰️ Satellite Relay (Advanced)
When all other networks fail, licensed amateur radio operators can relay messages via amateur satellites and the ISS.
Free options available:
🛸 ISS APRS — ISS relays short digital packets when overhead (no fee, requires amateur license)
📡 AMSAT CubeSats — Some support digital packet relay (see network tab)
🍎 iPhone Satellite — Emergency SOS and Messages on iPhone 14+ (no ARK control)
⚖️ Legal note: Amateur satellite relays in the US require an FCC amateur radio license. Encryption and third-party commercial traffic are prohibited under Part 97. Satellite relay in ARK is for public emergency text messages only.

❤️CPR Guide (Adult)
❤️
0
Compressions
CPR Steps:
  1. Check scene safety. Tap shoulder: "Are you OK?"
  2. Call 911 (or have someone call). Note time.
  3. Lay on back, tilt head back, lift chin.
  4. Check breathing for no more than 10 seconds.
  5. Place heel of hand on center of chest (between nipples).
  6. Push hard, fast: 2 inches deep at 100–120 per minute.
  7. Allow full recoil between compressions.
  8. After 30 compressions, give 2 rescue breaths if trained.
  9. Continue until help arrives or AED is ready.

🩹Wound Care
  1. Wash hands or use gloves before touching a wound.
  2. Apply firm, continuous pressure with clean cloth — do not lift to check for 10 min.
  3. If cloth soaks through, add more on top. Don't remove.
  4. Elevate the injured area above heart level if possible.
  5. Once bleeding slows, clean wound with clean water under pressure.
  6. Cover with clean bandage. Change if it gets wet or dirty.
  7. Signs of infection (days later): increasing redness, warmth, swelling, pus, red streaks, fever — seek medical help.
For arterial bleeding (bright red, spurting): apply tourniquet 2–3 inches above wound. Note the time. Do not remove. Get to medical help immediately.
