Welcome to ARK Crisis Suite

ARK is a fully offline-capable emergency communication and coordination tool. It works without internet, can run from a USB drive, and you can share it instantly with anyone who needs it.

  • Works 100% offline — no servers, no accounts required
  • Send this file via text, email, AirDrop, or USB to your neighbors
  • Coordinate with your neighborhood hub when in range
  • Manage medical triage, resources, and household status
⚡ This app requires no internet. All data is stored only on your device. Nothing is transmitted to any company or server unless you connect to a local hub.

Connect to a Local Hub

A Community Hub is ARK running on a local computer or Raspberry Pi nearby. When you're in range, your device syncs messages and resource updates automatically.

  • Works on any local WiFi — no internet needed
  • Your neighborhood coordinator should give you a Hub address
  • If you don't have one yet, skip this — you can add it later in Settings
💡 Setting up a hub? The ARK Hub software runs on any computer or Raspberry Pi. Download it from the same place you got this file, or ask your neighborhood coordinator.

Share ARK — Build Your Network

The more people have ARK, the stronger your community's resilience. Share this single HTML file with anyone who may need it — it contains the entire application.

  • Text message — Send the file as an attachment
  • AirDrop / Nearby Share — Works phone-to-phone in seconds
  • USB drive — Copy the file, hand the drive to anyone
  • Email — Attach and send like any document
  • QR code — Generate a code on the Share tab
✓ The whole app is one file. Sharing it = installing it. Recipients just open it in any browser — no app store, no account, no internet.

You're Ready

ARK is configured and ready for use. Here's a quick guide to the main tabs:

  • 🏠 Home — Status overview, quick actions, share ARK
  • 💬 Messages — Community messages; sync with hub when in range
  • 🌿 Survival — Water, food, shelter, fire, first aid checklists
  • 📦 Resources — Post what you have; find what others have nearby
  • 👥 Household — Track your household members and status
  • 🌐 Network — Peer discovery, hub connection, mesh status
  • 🏥 Medical — Triage, medications, hospital coordination
  • 🗺️ Map — Mark critical locations offline
  • 📻 Radio — Emergency frequency reference
  • ⚙️ Settings — All configuration, backup, and restore
💡 Tip: Tap Share ARK on the Home tab any time to share with someone new.
