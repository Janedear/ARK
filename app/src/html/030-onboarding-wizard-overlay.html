Welcome to ARK

When phones and the internet stop working, ARK links the devices around you into a small local network. Here is how:

  • One laptop becomes the hub.
  • Nearby phones join it over Wi-Fi.
  • Hubs pass messages on to each other, by network, radio, or a USB stick.
  • With no hub, ARK still works on this device: first aid guides, checklists, and your family plan.
What you enter stays on this device. It is only shared with a local hub if you choose to join one. No account. No company servers.
ARK is not an emergency service. Nobody monitors it. If you can reach 911 or your local emergency number, call them first.

Join a hub (optional)

An ARK Hub is a laptop or Raspberry Pi nearby that runs the ARK Hub program. Phones join it over Wi-Fi to share messages. A phone cannot be the hub by itself.

  • It works on local Wi-Fi. No internet needed.
  • The hub's address is shown on the hub's screen. The person running it can tell you.
  • No hub yet? Leave this empty and tap Continue. You can add one later.
ARK was opened from this address, so it is most likely your hub. It is filled in for you. Clear the box if you do not want to join it.
Want to run a hub yourself? You need a laptop or Raspberry Pi. Get the ARK Hub program from the same place you got ARK.
Step 3 of 5

Share ARK with people near you

ARK makes its network from people's devices, so it helps more when your neighbors have it too. The whole app is one file. You can pass it on with no internet:

  • AirDrop or Quick Share: phone to phone, in seconds
  • USB stick: copy the file and hand it over
  • Email or text: send the file as an attachment. This one needs internet or phone signal
  • QR code: if you are on a hub. Find it under Save & Share ARK
They open the file in any web browser. No app store, no account, no internet.
Step 4 of 5

You're ready

ARK is set up. Here is where things are:

  • Home: ask for help, say you are safe, checklists
  • Plan: your household, contacts and meeting places
  • Guides: first aid, water, shelter, fire
  • Messages: talk with people on your local hub
  • Hub: join a hub or check your connection
  • Ask Halo: answers with no internet
  • More at the top: Medical, Map, Radio, Resources, Settings
Tap Save & Share ARK at the top any time to give ARK to someone new.
