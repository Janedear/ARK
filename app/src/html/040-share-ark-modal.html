AirDrop / Nearby Share (Fastest)

Share phone-to-phone in seconds with no internet required.

  1. Download the file above first
  2. Open your Files app and find ARK-CRISIS-SUITE.html
  3. Long-press → Share → AirDrop (iPhone) or Nearby Share (Android)
  4. Select the recipient's device — done in under 10 seconds

USB Drive (No WiFi Needed)

The most reliable method — works even with no power grid or cellular service.

  1. Download ARK to your computer
  2. Copy ARK-CRISIS-SUITE.html to a USB drive
  3. Walk the USB drive to whoever needs it
  4. They plug in, open a browser, drag in the file — done
💡 Tip: Label the USB drive "ARK CRISIS SUITE — OPEN IN BROWSER". Put a text file on the drive with the same instructions. Keep copies on multiple drives.
