📡 Share ARK — Build Your Network

The more people have ARK, the stronger your community's resilience. Share this single file via text, AirDrop, USB, or email — no internet needed to receive it.

Why share? Each copy you share creates another node in your community network. When someone runs ARK and connects to the same local hub, your whole neighborhood gains resilience.
🛰️ Satellite Relay (Advanced)
When all other networks fail, licensed amateur radio operators can relay messages via amateur satellites and the ISS.
Free options available:
🛸 ISS APRS — ISS relays short digital packets when overhead (no fee, requires amateur license)
📡 AMSAT CubeSats — Some support digital packet relay (see network tab)
🍎 iPhone Satellite — Emergency SOS and Messages on iPhone 14+ (no ARK control)
⚖️ Legal note: Amateur satellite relays in the US require an FCC amateur radio license. Encryption and third-party commercial traffic are prohibited under Part 97. Satellite relay in ARK is for public emergency text messages only.
