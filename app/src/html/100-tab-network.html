📡Network

📡 How networking works: ARK makes its own internet from the devices around you. One laptop or Raspberry Pi runs the free ARK Hub program, and every phone nearby joins it over Wi-Fi — no outside internet needed. If someone is already running a hub, enter its address under Join a Hub. Everything else in ARK works without a hub — your plan, guides and notes are saved on this device.
