📶RST Signal Report System
Readability (1–5)
1 — Unreadable
2 — Barely readable
3 — Readable with difficulty
4 — Readable with no difficulty
5 — Perfectly readable
Signal Strength (1–9)
1 — Faint, barely perceptible
3 — Weak
5 — Fairly good
7 — Moderately strong
9 — Extremely strong
Common reports: "59" = perfectly readable, extremely strong. "33" = barely readable, weak. In an emergency, give a quick RST before asking for help.
