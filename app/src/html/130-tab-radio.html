NATO Phonetic Alphabet
· — ·Morse Code
● Playing…
SOS = · · · — — — · · · (3 short, 3 long, 3 short). Send it three times with a pause between each. Play makes a beep, flashes this screen and buzzes the phone. With no sound you can use a flashlight or a whistle.
Read Morse You Hear or See
Type . for short and - for long. Put a space between letters, and / between words. For example ... --- ... or .... . .-.. .-.. --- / .-- --- .-. .-.. -..

RST Signal Report System
Readability (1–5)
1 — Unreadable
2 — Barely readable
3 — Readable with difficulty
4 — Readable with no difficulty
5 — Perfectly readable
Signal Strength (1–9)
1 — Faint, barely perceptible
3 — Weak
5 — Fairly good
7 — Moderately strong
9 — Extremely strong
Common reports: "59" = perfectly readable, extremely strong. "33" = barely readable, weak. In an emergency, ask for help first. A signal report can wait.
