About ARK
In a crisis, ARK makes its own internet from the devices around you.
One laptop becomes the hub. Nearby phones join it over Wi-Fi. Hubs pass messages on to each other by network, radio or a USB stick.
This network is local. It needs an ARK Hub running on a laptop or a Raspberry Pi. A phone on its own cannot be the hub, and messages only reach people on those hubs.
Without a hub, this app still works by itself: the guides, your plan, your household details and the saved map are all kept on this device.
