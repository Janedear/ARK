Halo+ adds a larger AI model (Llama 3.2) that runs on this device. It needs internet once to download (about 600 MB to 1.8 GB), so do this before an emergency, on Wi-Fi. After that it works with no internet, as long as you open ARK the same way you did when you downloaded it. Each time you open ARK, tap Start Halo+ to switch it on. Try this once with Wi-Fi turned off before you rely on it. It needs a recent Chrome or Edge on a fairly new phone or computer.

Halo+ can answer more kinds of questions, but it can also get things wrong. The built-in Halo above always works, with or without this.
