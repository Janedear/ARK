For official services

Connect to ARK. Be there when the usual networks are down.

In a crisis, ARK makes its own internet from the devices around you. If your organisation helps people in an emergency, ask to link with it.

One laptop becomes the hub, nearby phones join it over Wi-Fi, and hubs pass messages on to each other. Linking lets you share verified local information on those hubs, or connect a hub your team runs. The network is local: it reaches only as far as the hubs people have set up. Every request is checked by a person before anything is linked.
