How it works

Ready in three steps. Do them today.

ARK only helps if it is already on your device when things go wrong. It takes a few minutes, at your pace.

Open ARK

  1. Open ARK and keep it

    It opens in your browser — no app store, no sign-up. Add it to your home screen, or use Save & Share ARK to keep a copy as a single file.

  2. Fill in your plan

    Add your household, emergency contacts and meeting places, then print the emergency card. Everything stays on your own device.

  3. Share it, and set up a hub

    Send ARK to family and neighbours. For messaging with no internet, put the free ARK Hub on one laptop and practise once.

Everything, with no internet

For official services

Emergency services and agencies: connect to ARK.

If you run emergency management, a responder team, a shelter, a utility or a community organisation, ask to link with ARK — to share verified local information, or to connect your own hub.

What ARK is — and is not

ARK is not an emergency service and nobody monitors it. In immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Messaging between phones needs an ARK Hub on a laptop on the same Wi-Fi. It reaches about as far as that Wi-Fi does, and its message board is public to everyone on it.

The guides draw on public sources such as the Red Cross, the American Heart Association, the CDC and FEMA. They are a reference, not a substitute for training or professional care.
